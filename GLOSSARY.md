# Stories of the Prophets

A study game in which the player witnesses the stories of the prophets as the Quran tells them.

## Story structure

**Chapter**:
One prophet's story as a whole, based on one surah (Yusuf (as) is Surah 12).
_Avoid_: level, story, episode

**Scene**:
One playable segment of a chapter, based on a continuous range of ayat, with its refs and certainty.
_Avoid_: level, stage

**Beat**:
One screen of text inside a scene; the player advances through a scene beat by beat. A beat is
either a narration beat or an ayah beat.
_Avoid_: page, slide, line, step

**Ayah beat**:
A beat that shows one complete ayah, in Arabic and English, exactly as it is in the Quran data;
never a fragment of an ayah.
_Avoid_: quote, dialogue line

**Narration**:
Text in a beat that is not Quran text: a faithful retelling that adds no detail beyond its sources.
_Avoid_: dialogue, script

**Witness**:
The ordinary person the player is in a scene, present in the story without being the prophet.
_Avoid_: hero, protagonist, player character

## Sources

**Ref**:
A pointer to the ayat a scene is based on, written `surah:ayah` or `surah:from-to`.
_Avoid_: verse id, citation

**Certainty**:
How a scene's content is known: from the Quran, from a named hadith, or from tradition (labelled in the game).
_Avoid_: confidence, reliability

## Player progress

**Ayat journal**:
The collection of ayat the player has unlocked by finishing scenes, browsable in Arabic and English.
_Avoid_: codex, log, notebook

**Source panel**:
The view of the exact ayat (and later, tafsir notes) a scene is based on.
_Avoid_: references screen, footnotes
