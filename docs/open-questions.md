# Open questions

Details that could not be verified and so are left out of the game. Each entry says what is
unverified and where it would be used.

## Quran data (ticket 01)

- **Translation licence.** The translation file carries only a short attribution header (name,
  translator, "Last Update: April 24, 2011", "Source: Tanzil.net"), not usage terms. The script appends
  it to `data/quran/LICENSE`. Whether Tanzil's Arabic terms are meant to cover the translation was not
  verified. Used by: `data/quran/LICENSE` and the in-game credits (which must also link to tanzil.net).

## Yusuf, scene at the well (12:19-20)

- **Who sold Yusuf (as) in 12:20.** The ayah says only "they sold him". Tafsir reportedly records
  two views (his brothers, or the caravan); this has not yet been checked against the text of Tafsir
  Ibn Kathir. The narration keeps the ayah's wording and does not name the seller. Used by: the
  narration beats of the well scene.
